# Cabinet inspection task

[Documentation guide](README.md) · [Research protocol](reproduction.md) ·
[Results](results.md)

## Task

A Unitree G1 starts upright at home in a storage bay. It must check the top
drawer of four cabinets shown on a static facility map, close any open drawer by
pushing the drawer front with a hand, leave closed drawers alone, return home
and declare completion. There is no confirmation button, fault-location oracle,
drawer-closing skill or navigation skill.

The protocol is `drawer-shutdown-v2`; the scenes are
`configs/shutdown-storage-<condition>.json`, generated by
[`shutdown_task.py`](../src/humanoid_agent/shutdown_task.py). All scenes share
geometry, instruction and map and differ only in the initial drawer states.

The [scripted reference](../src/humanoid_agent/runtime/shutdown_reference.py)
uses privileged state and is kept separate from model results. Its presence is
not evidence that a complete reference episode succeeded; the post-cleanup check
halted at a reach limit, as described in the reproduction guide.

## Scene and assets

The enclosure is NVIDIA's released Simple Room, loaded unchanged except for
pre-import overrides that deactivate its redundant ground plane and low table.
Its floor meshes are visual only. With `floor_support: flat-ground-collider` the
room's three floor colliders are disabled before Newton imports the stage, and
the robot stands on Isaac Lab's flat ground: an invisible 10 cm box with
friction 1 and no restitution. Walls and skirting still collide. The room's own
floor collider, a 1.8 cm bounding box with no physics material, made the
released SONIC policy spin and fall under command streams it tracked on flat
ground and in upstream's MuJoCo sim2sim; the
[results record](results.md) gives the diagnosis.

Four Sektion cabinets, `Props/Sektion_Cabinet/sektion_cabinet_instanceable.usd`,
are spawned through the `CABINET_CFG` articulation configuration of Isaac Lab's
cabinet task (the asset and articulation only, not that task's policy,
observations or rewards). They back onto the walls:

Home is the origin, facing +x. A 2.1 m block of three SimReady
`storage_locker_double_bench_b03` lockers (CC-BY-4.0) stands at x = 1.9 m
between home and cabinets A and B. The lockers are visual-only with hidden box
colliders. C and D stand behind home. No drawer front is visible from the start.

The top drawer is a prismatic joint along the cabinet's +x axis with limits
0–0.4 m; its front spans 0.64–0.76 m above the floor and its handle protrudes
3.8 cm. An open drawer starts pulled out 0.20 m. Upstream drives both drawers
toward closed with a 10 N/m position drive and 1 N·s/m damping. For the task
drawer the stiffness is zero and the upstream damping kept, so only contact can
close it; the other drawer and the doors keep their upstream drives. Drawer
positions are written once at reset and are physics-owned afterwards.

Newton imports the top drawer at 5.18 kg plus a 0.09 kg handle with no joint
friction, so it coasted and rebounded off its nearly elastic closed limit. The
task drawer therefore has 5 N dry slide friction (`drawer_static_friction_n`,
MuJoCo Warp `frictionloss`, a runner coefficient of 0.1 under its weight) and
`newton:limitDamping` of 460 N·s/m (`drawer_limit_damping_n_s_per_m`), critical
damping for Newton's 1e4 N/m limit stiffness and this mass. These are the only
physical deviations from the upstream articulation besides the removed
stiffness.

## Conditions

Eight conditions are reported: `none-open`, `open-A`, `open-B`, `open-C`,
`open-D`, `open-A-D`, `open-B-C` and `open-A-B`. The remaining pairs are
generated but were not scheduled. `balanced_condition_schedule` produces seeded
blocks containing every condition once; every model uses the same schedule.

## Observations and actions

Each model request contains:

- onboard RGB-D rendered from the measured torso pose, and a fixed external RGB
  view;
- robot proprioception and robot-only forward kinematics;
- read-only depth queries on the current onboard image;
- recent action feedback and the model's revisable subtask plan;
- the static facility map, generated from the frozen scene into
  `inputs/facility-map.png` and hashed into the episode protocol. It shows the
floor, cabinets, drawer fronts, lockers, home, the initial heading and a
metric grid, but neither drawer states nor a live robot position.

The model acts through SONIC's hybrid interface: native walking and posture
commands with numerical head and wrist pose keyframes, and binary Dex3 hand
presets. It chooses route, speed, facing, reach and timing; SONIC supplies gait,
balance and tracking. With `leashed_facing` the planner timeline holds the
commanded facing within 15° of the measured heading until it reaches the model's
requested facing, as the released gamepad turns by small stick increments. The
G1 has no actuated neck; the camera follows measured body motion only.

Simulator root coordinates, drawer joint positions, contact forces and evaluator
state are never model inputs.

## Independent scoring

The evaluator runs every 20 ms of physics and is separate from the model's
completion declaration.

- A drawer is closed and settled when its slide position is at most 2 cm and its
  speed at most 1 cm/s, held for one second.
- An initially closed drawer is disturbed if it ever opens more than 2 cm beyond
  its initial position, even if it is closed again.
- Filtered normal-force histories of all 44 G1 bodies against the drawer and
  handle shapes are split into Dex3 wrist/finger bodies and every other body;
contact counts above 0.5 N. Each closing displacement is attributed to the
latest contact source, so coasting after a push stays with that push. A drawer
is `closed_by_hand` when it was initially open, ended closed and settled, and
hand-attributed displacement accounts for its closure.
- Return home requires every drawer closed and settled, the pelvis floor
  projection within 25 cm of the start, and a supported standing hold for one
second: pelvis at least 0.65 m high and within 60° of upright, horizontal and
vertical speed at most 0.1 m/s, both feet supported, knees off the floor and
both knee angles at most 0.5 rad.

The physical criterion is all drawers closed and settled plus return home.
Success additionally requires the model's completion declaration.
`task-outcome.json` records, per drawer, initial, final and maximum positions,
closure, disturbance, hand attribution and contact durations, plus when physical
completion first occurred and when completion was declared. A physically
complete episode without a declaration is never counted as success. `none-open`
controls are reported separately from task attempts starting with open drawers.

These tolerances are local definitions for this asset. RLBench's CloseDrawer
task is prior art for drawer closure; its thresholds are not copied.

## Execution

The privileged reference runs through the production controller check:

```bash
uv run humanoid-agent --role native-controller-check \
  --task-scene configs/shutdown-storage-open-A-D.json \
  --diagnostic-camera on --output artifacts/storage-reference-NEW
```

`ShutdownReferenceControl` reads the frozen scene, measured root and hand points
and private drawer positions. It walks to a staging point straight out from each
drawer, raises both hands to the drawer front, walks forward with the hands
held, extends the hands to finish, eases off, backs away and re-checks the
drawer, retrying if it reopened, then returns home. It never writes simulator
state. Use `--diagnostic-motion standing` for the no-interaction check.

Model episodes use the ordinary episode command with a storage scene; see
[Operations](reproduction.md). Freeze the scene, cameras, map, prompts,
evaluator, time budget and source before comparing models.
